About

A measured approach to capital and enterprise

Dr. Rostyslav Prus is an entrepreneur and investor based in the Kuala Lumpur area. Of Ukrainian origin, he currently leads R.P.V. Holdings Ltd as his principal company, at the intersection of strategic investment, capital preservation, and financial education.

As Founder & Director of R.P.V. Holdings Ltd, a Labuan holding company established in 2026, he focuses on strategic investments and the long-term preservation of capital. His earlier career was in investment banking and capital markets in Labuan, Malaysia. He holds a Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) from the Swiss School of Management (2021) and a Doctor of Philosophy in Business Administration from City University Malaysia (2026).

What I do

Investments & holdings

Through R.P.V. Holdings Ltd (Labuan, Malaysia), his principal company, he oversees a private holding company created for strategic investments and the long-term preservation of capital. This site is a personal profile and is not an invitation to invest.

Investment banking & capital markets

He previously served as Managing Director of RM Investment Bank Ltd (April 2021 – April 2026) and as Executive Managing Director of RoboMarkets Asia Ltd (May 2019 – 2023). He is a co-founder and former Deputy Chairman of the Labuan Investment Banks Association (LIBA), until 2026.

Financial education & RPV Markets

RPV Markets is being prepared as a platform for trading rebates and a financial education academy.

Professional forums

He takes part in professional gatherings on investment and entrepreneurship, and spoke at Digital Finance Asia in Kuala Lumpur in November 2025.
